✎ Identity Tip of the Day

Review your OAuth tokens: especially those older than 6 months or with broad scopes. Identify and revoke any tokens accessing multiple resources with the same key, prioritizing those with high-sensitivity access. This can help mitigate potential breaches like Kenya’s eCitizen incident.
