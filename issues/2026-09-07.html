✎ Identity Tip of the Day

If you manage AI agents, review their least-privilege settings: look for any permissions set broadly by default, then tighten to 'deny' first. AI agents' rapid actions can be unpredictable; it's crucial to have runtime monitoring that goes beyond initial verification.
