Gopass: Open-source command-line password manager for teams↗

Gopass is a command-line tool for managing passwords, designed for team use. It's a more secure, trackable alternative that encrypts passwords with GPG and synchronizes them via git. This is handy for teams preferring open-source solutions over commercial products. The catch: it requires some command-line skills, which may steepen the learning curve for non-technical users.

✎ Identity Tip of the Day

Audit your authentication logs for login attempts by 'trusted' email domains that aren't whitelisted internal systems. These are often missed in alerts and can mask phishing or credential stuffing attempts under a false sense of security.
