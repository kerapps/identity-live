Cybercriminals are building phishing pages that exist only inside victims’ browsers↗

Phishing pages are being crafted directly inside victims' browsers using blob URLs. This attack method bypasses traditional web server delivery, making detection harder and exploiting the trust users have in their own browsers. The catch: defense requires focusing on browser-level security measures and user education about content origins.
